How it works

Four steps
to the perfect shot.

01

Upload your shots

Add two or more photos from the same moment. The more options, the better the result.

02

Faces are found & matched

Postable detects every face across every photo and groups them by person — automatically.

03

Choose the best version

For each person, tap the face you love most. You're in full control of every choice.

04

Download & share

Postable composites your selections into one seamless image. Download and share instantly.

Built different

Precision geometry.
Not generative AI.

Most photo tools send your images to a cloud server and run them through a black box. Postable is different in every way that matters.

Your photos never leave your device

Everything runs entirely inside your browser. No uploads. No servers. No account needed. We literally cannot see your photos.

Geometry, not AI

Faces are composited using affine transformations — mathematical alignment based on the geometry of your facial landmarks. No hallucinations. No distortion. Just math.

Nothing stored. Ever.

Close the tab and it's all gone. No cookies, no local storage, no analytics. Postable has no idea you were ever here.

Face detection runs on a model that is downloaded to your browser once and cached locally. After that, it works offline.
